Built in Kisumu,
for Kisumu.

The Kisumu Ruby Community was founded in 2026 by a group of Ruby developers who wanted more than online tutorials. They wanted a local space to learn, build, and grow together. What started as informal meetups aims to grow into a thriving community of engineers, students, and entrepreneurs united by a love for Ruby and Rails.

Our mission is to grow the Ruby ecosystem in Western Kenya by creating opportunities for developers at every level to connect, collaborate, and ship real software.

# mission.rb

"Learn together.

Build real things.

Grow the ecosystem."

- Kisumu Ruby Community, est. 2026
